```text

```

Cause: `sail-lint` always called `std::time::Instant::now()` in `lint_controller_source` / `lint_sail_template_source` (profiling path), even when `SAIL_LINT_PROFILE` was unset. Wasm has no platform clock.

**Fix:** `crates/sail/lint/src/lib.rs` — gate Instant profiling behind `cfg(not(target_arch = "wasm32"))` (same pattern as `crates/sail/compiler/src/check/mod.rs`).
