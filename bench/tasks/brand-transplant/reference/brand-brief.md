# Brand brief — Northledger

**Category:** B2B fintech (treasury ops for mid-market)

**Personality:** trustworthy, calm, precise — never flashy, never playful.

**Color:** deep forest green as primary brand color. Supporting neutrals are
cool slate greys. Accent sparingly with a muted gold for CTAs only.

**Type:** strong hierarchy; headings medium-weight, body quiet. Prefer
system / product fonts over display novelty.

**Density:** comfortable enterprise density — room to breathe, not sparse
marketing whitespace and not spreadsheet-tight.

**Deliverable for this task:** a product page (hero + three features + footer)
that a stranger would identify as “serious green fintech,” expressed through
the token/theme system rather than one-off paint.
